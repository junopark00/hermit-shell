Third-party licenses
====================

Shell is distributed under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (LICENSE next to shell.exe).
NOTICE lists every third-party component and where it is used. This folder holds the license
texts of the components that are compiled into shell.exe or shipped with it.

  GCC-Runtime-Library-Exception-3.1.txt (the GPL v3 text is LICENSE)

  tray.txt, nanors.txt, libdisplaydevice.txt, nvapi.txt, nv-codec-headers.txt

  Boost.txt, OpenSSL.txt, curl.txt, libssh2.txt, libpsl.txt, libidn2.txt, libunistring.txt,
  libiconv.txt, brotli.txt, zstd.txt, zlib.txt (zlib1.dll), opus.txt, miniupnpc.txt, MinHook.txt,
  libvpl.txt, nlohmann-json.txt, winpthreads.txt, mingw-w64-runtime.txt, GCC-runtime.txt
